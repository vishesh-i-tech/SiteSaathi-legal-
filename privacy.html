SiteSaathi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

SiteSaathi is a construction site management application for contractors and site supervisors.

Information You Enter

The app may store information you enter, such as your name, mobile number, site details, labour records, wages, materials, machinery, vendor dues, reminders and client payments.

Local Storage and Offline Use

SiteSaathi is designed for offline-first use. Records may be stored locally on your device. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or app session may be able to access locally stored information.

Google Account and Google Drive

Connecting a Google account is optional for supported backup features. If you connect Google, the app may request permission to access files it creates or that you explicitly select, depending on the permissions shown during sign-in. Google account information and Drive data are handled according to the permissions you grant.

Data Sharing

SiteSaathi does not require you to connect the developer's personal Google account. You should connect only your own Google account. Do not share your PIN or backup password with others.

Security and Backups

We take steps to protect app data, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device secure and maintain backups where available.

Your Choices

You can choose whether to connect Google for supported backup features. You are responsible for reviewing the data stored on your device and in your Google Drive.

Children's Privacy

SiteSaathi is intended for contractors and site supervisors, not children.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as SiteSaathi's features change. The latest version will be published on this page.
